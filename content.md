# Content — how I build an audience

Last updated: 1 November 2026

## Why
Content is how Blue 34 gets found: shoot once, reach many, instead of one-to-one outreach. I'm learning in public, and the learning is part of the value, because AI is moving too fast for anyone to have it all worked out. The thread running through it: helping people find the new coalface.

## The non-negotiable
**I write everything myself.** LinkedIn posts and articles, Substack articles and Notes are always mine. AI can give me ideas, angles and prompts and push me for thoughts, but never drafts the words.

AI may draft short captions for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Keep them short, and I approve every one before it goes out.

## Platforms and tone
- **LinkedIn:** business and network focused.
- **TikTok:** more playful.
- **Facebook:** business oriented.
- **Instagram:** a different look; the place to drive people to a landing page, quiz or wider outreach (to be worked out).
- **Substack:** where I indulge: sport, some AI, films and things I find interesting. Positive, personal, a place for the writing to flourish. Target 3 Notes a week minimum, ideally 1 a day, plus long-form.

## Off limits
Nothing too personal on LinkedIn. Substack can be more personal. Family stays off: I may speak warmly about my wife and children, but the content is never about them.

## Production
I shoot when an idea lands, often first thing in the morning. Footage goes into Claude Code on my computer, where a video-edit skill does the cut. Each finished piece should log one line (platform, date, link) to a shared content log, so the team knows what's gone out.

Equipment and more eye-catching formats are on the list, especially for TikTok.

## The content desk (from 1 November 2026)
A **Head of Content** now runs content, reporting to the Chief of Staff, with LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook editors. It works in Claude Code on my computer; working files (strategy, schedule, clip inventory, tutorial run sheets) live in the `content` folder of my Staffing directory there. It stays in sync with the Chief of Staff through Notion, Todoist and The Build.

- **Brand:** Blue 34, with @blue34digital as the handle if necessary. PITCH uses Tasks.
- **The Build** (claude.ai) is the planning document and wins any clash. The **Content Log** in Notion is the record of results.
- **LinkedIn results** are recorded or acted on only from day 7. Earlier numbers are incomplete and uneven.
- **Time:** a proposed 6 hours a week, one batch shoot on Mondays from 9 November. Content gives way first.

Dates that matter:
- Mon 2 to Fri 6 Nov: five LinkedIn tutorials, one a day; Instagram built alongside.
- Thu 5 Nov: Release 1 launch post in the morning, tutorial in the afternoon.
- Mon 9 to Fri 13 Nov: Build Week re-run on TikTok and Facebook; Instagram schedule starts.
- Fri 13 Nov: first review, LinkedIn video versus written mix.
- Fri 20 Nov: two-week review: keep, cut or change each channel.

## The editorial team (run by the Chief of Staff; now the Head of Content's desk)
- **Ideas desk:** one ideas bank, tagged by platform and theme; the best five come up each Monday.
- **Platform desk:** suggests the angle for each platform from one master piece; drafts only the short captions allowed above.
- **Notes desk:** turns each week's material into Note prompts (observation, question, behind the scenes, one-line take, recommendation); one prompt a day in the morning note.
- **Analytics desk:** a weekly scorecard per platform, now produced by the Head of Content's Friday review (LinkedIn from day-7 readings, other platforms from my Friday screenshots or exports); says what worked, what didn't and what to try next. Content-sourced conversations are tagged "Content" in the Pipeline.

## 90-day goal (to late January 2027)
Four figures of views and impressions per day, ideally per platform. Generous measure for now. Real success: people asking for more, the content shifting how people see me, and inbound conversations that start from it.
